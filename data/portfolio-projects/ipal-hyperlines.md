## Overview

iPal is a decentralized marketplace for datasets, using Web3 smart contracts to mint NFTs for subscription access on Ethereum. Hyperlines builds on that pipeline, taking it from raw data through analysis to financial and crypto trading signals, and adding automation and execution on top.
