### Overview

How are artists, artworks, institutions, and historical events connected, and how have these relationships evolved over time?

**ArtVis** is an award-winning suite of interactive visualization tools that brings these connections to life. By combining art history with network visualization, ArtVis enables researchers to explore complex relationships, discover hidden patterns, and trace how the art world has evolved over time.

### Recognition

ArtVis received the **2025 Digital Humanities Award** in the **Best Tool / Suite of Tools** category, recognizing its contribution to making complex historical information more accessible and explorable.

### Collaboration

ArtVis is a research collaboration between **TU Wien** and the **University of Vienna**, supported by the **Austrian Science Fund (FWF)**.
