### Overview

A résumé tells the story of someone's professional journey, but comparing multiple résumés can be surprisingly difficult. Different formats, overlapping experiences, and diverse skill sets make it challenging to see the bigger picture.

**CV3** reimagines how we explore and compare résumés through interactive visualizations. By bringing together professional histories, skills, timelines, and geographic information, the tool helps users discover similarities, identify differences, and better understand candidates' experiences and career paths.

### Exploring Careers Visually

Rather than relying on static documents, CV3 introduces a visual approach to exploring professional profiles. Interactive skill trees reveal shared and unique competencies, while timelines and maps illustrate when and where careers have developed.

The system also supports comparing and ranking candidates against specific job requirements, helping recruiters explore their options and make more informed decisions.

### Research

CV3 was presented at **EuroVis 2019** and published in *Computer Graphics Forum*. The research explores how interactive visualization can support complex comparison and decision-making tasks, with the approach evaluated through a study involving professional recruiters.
