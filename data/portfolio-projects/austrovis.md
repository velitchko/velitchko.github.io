### Overview

Great ideas often emerge when people come together.

**AustroVis** is a recurring workshop series that connects Austria's data visualization and visual analytics community. Bringing together researchers, students, and practitioners from institutions across the country, it provides a welcoming space to exchange ideas, share research, and build connections beyond institutional boundaries.

### Building a Local Community

Since its first edition in 2023, AustroVis has grown into a continuing initiative, with workshops hosted by different institutions across Austria. Each edition creates opportunities to discover ongoing research, discuss shared challenges, and strengthen collaboration within the local visualization community.

### My Involvement

As a co-organizer, I contribute to both the workshops and the infrastructure that keeps the community connected. This includes developing and maintaining the website, managing registration and mailing lists, coordinating communication, and supporting community outreach.

I also organized the **sixth AustroVis workshop at TU Wien in February 2026**, contributing to the continued growth of the initiative.
