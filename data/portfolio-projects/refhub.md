### Overview

Research is about connecting ideas, but keeping track of publications, references, and related work can quickly become overwhelming.

**RefHub** is an open-source research platform designed to make organizing and exploring academic knowledge easier. It brings publications, citation networks, and research collections together in one place, helping researchers discover connections, organize their literature, and share knowledge with others.

### Beyond Reference Management

RefHub goes beyond traditional reference management by connecting everyday research activities with modern tools and workflows. From browser extensions and command-line interfaces to AI-powered integrations, it aims to make research knowledge accessible not just to people, but also to the tools and intelligent agents that assist them.

Built around openness, interoperability, and automation, RefHub explores a more connected and flexible way of managing research.
