### Overview

What if creators could share and monetize their knowledge without relying on traditional platforms or intermediaries?

**IPAL** began as a decentralized marketplace for exchanging knowledge. Built around the idea of digital knowledge vaults, the platform allows creators to organize and share valuable information while maintaining control over who can access it and under what conditions.

Using blockchain technology, IPAL introduces transparent access rights, automated subscriptions, and direct payments between creators and their audiences.

### Rethinking Knowledge Exchange

At the heart of IPAL is a system of knowledge vaults: collections of information that creators can make available through free or paid subscriptions.

Rather than relying on conventional accounts and centralized payment systems, the platform uses blockchain-based access credentials to manage permissions and subscriptions. Smart contracts automatically handle ownership verification, access expiration, and revenue distribution between contributors.

### From Knowledge to Intelligence

IPAL laid the technical and conceptual foundation for a broader vision: a community-driven ecosystem in which people can contribute, exchange, and build upon valuable information.

This direction eventually evolved into **Hyperlines**, extending the original marketplace concept toward financial intelligence, community-generated trading signals, and automated trading workflows.
