### Overview

Museums and cultural institutions preserve enormous collections of artworks, photographs, and historical artifacts. But how can we explore these collections in ways that reveal more than individual objects?

**PolyCube** reimagines the exploration of cultural heritage through interactive visualizations. By bringing together geography, time, categories, and relationships, the system helps visitors and researchers discover patterns, trace historical developments, and better understand the connections within cultural collections.

### Exploring Collections from Different Perspectives

PolyCube combines maps, timelines, networks, and three-dimensional space-time cubes to provide multiple perspectives on the same collection.

Users can explore where objects originated, how collections evolved, and how artifacts relate to one another. By switching between interconnected views, they can uncover relationships that might remain hidden when browsing traditional digital archives.

### Research

PolyCube investigates how interactive visualization can help people develop a more complete understanding of complex cultural information.

The research explores how different visual representations influence the way people interpret and connect information, with user studies examining the benefits of coordinated views. Related work also investigates how to visualize changes in cultural categories over time, helping reveal the evolving structure of historical collections.

### Collaboration

PolyCube was an interdisciplinary research collaboration between **Danube University Krems** (now University for Continuing Education Krems) and **TU Wien**, supported by the **Austrian Science Fund (FWF)**.
