### Overview

How has music shaped the identity of Vienna, and what can historical celebrations tell us about the city's cultural and social evolution?

**Interactive Music Mapping Vienna (IMMV)** explores Vienna's musical heritage through interactive visualizations of public festivities from 1945 onward. By bringing together historical records, people, places, and events, the project uncovers connections and patterns that reveal how music has influenced the city's cultural identity over time.

### Circular: Exploring History Through Visualization

As part of the project, we developed **Circular**, an interactive visualization tool that offers a new way to explore complex historical networks.

Using a distinctive circular layout, it brings together people, locations, events, and their relationships in a single interactive view. Researchers can trace connections, follow changes over time, and discover patterns that might otherwise remain hidden in traditional historical records.

### Research

Our work resulted in the publication **"Gone Full Circle: A Radial Approach to Visualize Event-Based Networks in Digital Humanities"** in *Visual Informatics* (2021).

Developed through close collaboration between visualization researchers and historians, Circular explores how interactive visual representations can support historical research. The approach was evaluated with domain and visualization experts, demonstrating its potential to make complex historical relationships more accessible and explorable.

### Collaboration

Interactive Music Mapping Vienna was an interdisciplinary research collaboration between **TU Wien** and the **Music and Arts University of the City of Vienna (MUK)**, supported by the **Austrian Science Fund (FWF)**.
