### Overview

What happens when data visualization meets games and play?

**VisGames** is an ongoing initiative that brings together researchers, designers, and enthusiasts to explore how playful experiences can make data more engaging, accessible, and collaborative. Through interactive games, hands-on workshops, and creative experimentation, it explores new ways of understanding information, sharing ideas, and solving problems together.

### Learning Through Play and More

Games can do much more than make learning fun. VisGames explores their potential as tools for creative thinking, collaborative problem-solving, and engaging people in the design and interpretation of data.

From card and board games to digital experiences and collaborative design challenges, the initiative provides a space to experiment with new ideas, exchange perspectives, and discover unexpected applications of visualization.

### Growing a Community

**What began as a workshop at EuroVis 2025 and expanded into 2026 is now an ongoing international initiative**, bringing researchers, designers, and visualization enthusiasts together across successive editions.

Beyond the annual workshops, VisGames aims to cultivate a lasting community around playful approaches to visualization. By collecting games, sharing resources, and encouraging collaboration, the initiative continues to create opportunities for experimentation, exchange, and new research directions.
