### Overview

Writing projects tend to spread across folders, editors, comments, and one-off AI chats. **Vellum** is a private, self-hostable workspace intended to bring the core of that work back together: a project, its Markdown files, and a focused surface for drafting and review.

### A quiet writing surface

Projects contain a small collection of files rather than an all-purpose productivity system. Writers can switch between documents, edit Markdown, preview the rendered result, export files, and retain a history of changes without leaving the workspace.

### Human and agent collaboration

Vellum is designed for deliberate collaboration with agents, not a generic chat pane bolted onto an editor. Its companion bridge exposes bounded, file-scoped work through an OpenAI-compatible interface, so assistance can be connected where it is useful while the writing project remains the source of truth.

### Current direction

The project is being developed as a private MVP with a calm, low-friction interface and an eventual path for others to self-host it. The screenshots show a seeded local demonstration workspace rather than private writing.
