### Overview

Financial markets never stop moving, but making sense of market activity and acting on it at the right moment can be challenging.

**Hyperlines** is a community-driven trading platform that brings together real-time market visualization, shared insights, and automated trading. Built on Hyperliquid, it helps traders monitor market movements, discover opportunities, and turn their strategies into actionable decisions through an intuitive, interactive interface.

### From Insights to Action

Hyperlines connects market analysis directly with trading execution.

Users can interact with live price charts, draw custom price boundaries, and define conditions that trigger notifications or trading actions. When prices cross these thresholds, the platform can deliver Telegram alerts or execute predefined strategies, helping traders respond to market movements without constantly monitoring charts.

Through wallet-based authentication and decentralized trading infrastructure, the platform brings market monitoring, strategy management, and order execution together in one place.

### Community-Driven Intelligence

Building on the foundations of IPAL, Hyperlines extends the concept of knowledge vaults into a marketplace for trading insights and signals.

Instead of sharing general knowledge, community members can contribute market research, datasets, indicators, and trading strategies. These contributions can become building blocks for more informed decisions and increasingly automated trading workflows.

By combining shared intelligence with practical trading tools, Hyperlines explores how collaborative knowledge can be transformed into actionable strategies.
